## Architecture Principles

**Statement**- The following principles are intended to guide the evolution and growth of the engineering community within the company.

### Principle #1 - Openness by default

**Statement** -
Design and engineer for open collaboration, patterns and practices

**Rationale** -
The more open we work, the easier our patterns and practices are seen, adopted, and improved. This leads to faster community growth.

**Implications** -
We're setting the norm to allow internal and external colleagues to have access to all information that may be relevant today or tomorrow.

    All colleagues shall have
    - free access to project documentation
    - free access to code
    - free access to meetings and group conversations, their transcripts and recordings
Reasons to undisclose information exist, but should be limited to:

- *protecting the confidence of a partner/customer/colleague*
- *personnel information*
- *data protection, e.g. GDPR*

### Principle #3 - Simplify by design

**Statement** -
Design and engineer for simplicity.

**Rationale** -
Simple concepts and programs are easier to communicate, build, deploy, operate, and evolve thus requiring lower costs.

**Implications** -
Simple is hard and requires a better understanding of the problem space, available technologies, experience with previous designs to eliminate unneeded complexity and ongoing effort to fight entropy over time.
Avoid over-engineering today to pay off in the future at the same time balance to provide for the easy integration of near term capabilities.
Avoid premature optimization and allow the design to emerge from known implementations.
Focus on programs that are directly supporting functionality that provides value that is differentiating.
Strive for the Simplest Option:
Of two otherwise equal solutions, the simpler is usually better.

### Principle #4 - Design for inclusivity

**Statement** -
Diversity, equity, inclusion and belonging is a top priority and always top of mind.

**Rationale** -
Diversity, equity and inclusion has to be an integral part of all our concepts from the very beginning and cannot be bolted on afterwards.

**Implications** -
In a world where our talents are our most precious assets, we can't afford to exlcude groups of people, marginalise them or otherwise unintentionally ignore any parts of the global workforce. Instead we want to seek our otherness, avoid limitations of sameness and strive to create teams that reach the highest level of creativity and performance through their diversity.
